# Starlancer — Capital Ships & Installations

Carriers, cruisers, command ships, transports and fortified installations of both navies, from the
[Starlancer Wiki](https://starlancer.fandom.com/) (see [credit](README.md#-source--credit)).

Conventions: Alliance ships carry the **ANS** prefix (Alliance Naval Ship), Coalition ships the
**CS** prefix (with the native designation **КК/КС** on Russian vessels). Carrier displacements are
in metric tons. The infobox "Armament" field is a relative tier (Light / Medium / Heavy), **not** a
hard weapon count — explicit counts exist for only a handful of ships and are given where known.

> **Spelling note for RE:** the game/`.DTE` files spell two ships differently from the wiki —
> **Ramases** (game) = *CS Rameses* (wiki), and **Boridin** (game) = *CS Borodin* (wiki). The game
> spelling is authoritative for cross-referencing decoded mission files; see
> [`campaign.md`](campaign.md) and [`campaign-flow.md`](../campaign-flow.md).

## Alliance (ANS)

### Player home carriers

**ANS Reliant** — *Class 4 Carrier* (45,000 t, crew 292, 25–35 craft, comm. 2125). The player's home
carrier for the first half of the campaign. A 35-year-old Lunar/Europa Conflict veteran,
decommissioned to an armed transport and then recalled and refitted with **eight level-6 twin-laser
turrets** (the only explicit weapon count for any Alliance ship). Cramped and obsolete but effective.
Commanded by **Captain Foster**; home of the **45th Volunteers / Tigers** (the player's unit).
**Lost in Mission 15 (June 24, 2161)** when Foster rams it into the CS Volga.

**ANS Yamato** — *Class 1 Carrier* (88,000 t, crew 587, 40–55 craft, comm. 2159). The **largest and
newest** Alliance carrier and the player's home for the second half. Built for power projection; base
of the **409th Ronin** (and later the 45th Tigers). Destroys the CS Zakov and CS Kirov early, raids
deep into Coalition lines with the Washington, and fights to the final mission alongside the
Intrepid against CS Borodin.

### Other named Alliance vessels

- **ANS Churchill** — a **Kestrel-class Fleet Destroyer** (15,000 t, crew 131, 6 craft). In Mission
  19, escorted by the Pirates, it arrives to evacuate prisoners from the captured *Saladin*.
- **ANS Ulysses** — a **Starstream-class civilian liner** (39,000 t), reassigned for VIP transport;
  **unarmed**. In Mission 6 it carries officers to a war council and is attacked.
- **ANS Illustrious** — base carrier of the 606th Black Aces & 705th Cobras *(mentioned only)*.
- **ANS Roma** — base carrier of the 559th Hornets *(mentioned only)*.
- **ANS Valiant** — an Alliance carrier, **destroyed by the CS Pukov** *(mentioned only)*.
- **ANS Mantis** — a command ship whose **destroyed remains** are recovered in Mission 3.

### Support / small craft (not capital ships, included for completeness)

- **Galahad** — fast Alliance torpedo bomber (8 t); 45th Gamma wing.
- **Hades** — heavier successor torpedo bomber (12 t); Bravo Wing, 45th Tigers; 4 daranium torpedoes.
- **Stork** — satellite/minelayer (1,100 t); Mission 4 escort.
- **Lonestar Shuttle** — Alliance transport shuttle (25 t); background workhorse.

### Unused / model-only Alliance ships

**ANS Alsace** (France), **ANS Falco** (Italy), **ANS Intruder** (Japan), **ANS Goodwill** (UK), and
**Goliath** — untextured models or absent from the game; no stats. National-flagship designs that
never made it in.

### Cruisers

The **Tornado-class** Class 2 cruisers (25,000 t, crew 151, comm. 2108) are 50-year-old raiders armed
with **7 laser turrets, 1 flak/main gun, and 44 torpedo tubes**, carrying 10 craft:

- **CS Kirov** (Киров) — Mission 2, with Zakov; both destroyed by the Yamato.
- **CS Morzov** (Морзов) — massacred a Ganymede refugee convoy; destroyed in Mission 16.
- **CS Kiev** (Киев) — destroyed by the Yamato in Mission 15; also appears (×3) in Trial Mission 1.
- **CS Shinnik** (Шинник) — rides with the Second Fleet, Mission 21.

The **Cyclone-class** Class 2 cruisers (23,200 t, comm. 2159) are faster escorts that double as small
carriers (10 craft):

- **CS Zakov** (Заков) — reinforces Kirov in Mission 2; both destroyed by the Yamato.
- **CS Yevstafiy** (Евстафий) — intervenes at the Czar (M11, invulnerable there); killed at M15.
- **CS Kresta** (Креста) — guarded a prison ship off Saturn; never actually encountered.

### Installations / forts

| Installation | Type | Role |
|--------------|------|------|
| **CS Borodin** (Бородин) | Fort / battle station | Forward command center of the outer-Sol fleets; base of the **Ravens**. Mounts a Dark-Reign-class ion cannon; three winged sections can detach and warp-evacuate. **Final mission (24) objective**, holding **Kulov and Ivan Petrov**. *(Game: "Boridin".)* |
| **Kronstadt** (Кроншадт) | Fort / depot | Deep-space storage & resupply; raided by the Yamato in Mission 22. |
| **Stalag** | Storage facility | Central warp-fuel-cell storage for the frontier; destroyed in Mission 12. |
| **Dark Reign** | Orbital ion cannon | Scaled-up ion cannon defending Titan; **one-shots a capital ship** (kills the ANS Victorious). Destroyed in Mission 18. |
| **Latov Asteroid Observation Base** | Observation base | Coordinated the Coalition's warp-strike attacks; destroyed in Mission 10. |

> The **Coalition fleet structure** for cross-referencing: the recurring nemeses are the **Black
> Guard** carrier **CS Pukov** and the command station **CS Borodin**; the campaign's three ion
> cannons are at **Fort Vanguard** (M13, a captured one), the **Dark Reign** (M18), and **CS Borodin**
> (M24). See [`weapons-and-progression.md`](weapons-and-progression.md#ion-cannons).
