# Starlancer — Character Roster

The 53 named characters of the Sol War, from the [Starlancer Wiki](https://starlancer.fandom.com/)
(see [credit](README.md#-source--credit)). The player flies in the **45th Tigers** (a.k.a. 45th
Volunteers); generic recruitable wingmen with no scripted plot fate are marked **45th wingman
(generic)** — they each have a callsign and a one-line background but no story arc.

## Key characters

The people who drive the campaign:

- **Vladimir Kulov** *(Coalition)* — the principal antagonist. A ruthlessly ambitious figure who rose
  through the Secret Police and Political Council; he masterminded the Deimos Massacre and the whole
  first strike. His flagship is the **CS Volga**; gallery art shows him escaping in a pod. *(Captured
  alive in Mission 17; present at the final battle.)*
- **Captain Robert Foster** *(Alliance)* — the **player's first CO**. A 57-year-old UK veteran who
  came out of retirement after both his sons were killed at the war's outbreak and founded the **45th
  Volunteers**. He awards the player their medals, and **dies ramming the ANS Reliant into the CS
  Volga** (Mission 15).
- **Captain Yoshinobu Mukai** *(Alliance)* — captain of the **ANS Yamato** and the youngest Academy
  graduate in history; becomes the **player's CO** after Foster's death.
- **Maria Enriquez "Red Fox"** *(Alliance)* — the squadron leader who **briefs the player** on every
  mission, and occasionally joins the fight.
- **Mike Horrigan "Moose"** *(Alliance)* — the player's **back-seat co-pilot in every mission** (a
  nod to *Top Gun*'s "Goose").
- **Mark Bannister "Bandit"** & **Linc Stevenson "Diceman"** *(Alliance)* — the player's key
  wingmen, who rise to wing leader in turn as the campaign progresses.
- **Brad Callan "Viper"** *(Alliance → traitor)* — the 45th's first wing leader; **fakes his death**
  (Aug 15, 2160) and defects to McGann's pirates.
- **Col. John McGann "Specter"** *(Alliance → rogue)* — Cobras CO who **goes rogue** in Mission 5 and
  turns pirate; killed at Fort Vanguard (Mission 13).
- **Col. Pierre Besson** *(Alliance traitor)* — the intelligence mole who leaked Alliance fleet
  positions, enabling the first strike; turned in by his wife and **executed by firing squad**
  (Apr 19, 2161).
- **Ivan Petrov "Chekan / The Butcher"** & **Nicolai Petrov "Incubus"** *(Coalition)* — brothers who
  lead the elite **Black Guard** from the CS Pukov. Ivan is the Republic's premier ace, named for
  executing POWs on Mars; the unstable Nicolai is his wingman. **Nicolai is killed July 11, 2161**;
  Ivan survives and flees.
