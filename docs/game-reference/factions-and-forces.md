# Starlancer — Factions, Squadrons & Locations

The two powers of the Sol War, their elite units, all 27 fighter squadrons, and the war's
installations — from the [Starlancer Wiki](https://starlancer.fandom.com/) (see
[credit](README.md#-source--credit)). For the broad overview and Sol geography see the
[index](README.md#the-two-powers); for the ships these units fly from, see
[`capital-ships.md`](capital-ships.md).

## The two powers

### Western Alliance

A supranational union of Western nations (member states: USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan,
Spain). The most technologically advanced power in history — it colonized much of Sol, terraformed
Mars and Europa, and built the system's automated zero-G / nanotech industry. A century of colonial
wars (the Lunar Conflicts, the Europa Uprising of 2150–52, the Mars Wars of 2154) left it war-weary,
so it agreed to the **Balma Treaty** at Base Kennedy and stood its fleets down — whereupon the
Coalition attacked (June 2160). The Franco-Italian fleets were destroyed at Deimos; Earth, Mars and
the Jupiter moons fell; ~45% of the fleet rallied at **Triton**. The Alliance fought back to win
Outer Sol within two years, but the war became a century-long stalemate it ultimately lost at Luna
(~2261), escaping Sol in five sleeper ships (the setup for *Freelancer*). Generally maintains
military law and honorable conduct.

### Eastern Coalition

A newly-formed union of non-Western nations, built from three blocs:

- **Eastern Republic** — the former USSR and its republics (Russia); supplies most of the named
  carriers and the Black Guard.
- **Tigris Confederation** — Middle Eastern states; the Saracens, Black Sun and Scorpions.
- **East Asian Federation (EAF)** — the Golden Warriors; the Azan/Haidar/Karak/Salin fighters.

Left behind technologically and confined largely to Venus and Mercury, the East united to avoid
permanent second-rate status, and used the Balma Treaty as a ruse for a perfidious first strike.
Governed by the **Political Council** backed by the **Coalition Secret Police**, and led by Admiral
**Vladimir Kulov**, who masterminded the attack. Notorious for total-war conduct — targeting
hospitals, civilian convoys and colonies; a "take no prisoners" policy; torture; and floating
concentration camps like the prison ship *Saladin*. Despite this, no shortage of volunteers from
satellite states (the **Sabers** are an all-volunteer/mercenary unit). Wins the century-long war —
and is annihilated in the scrapped Nomad ending.

## Elite units (Coalition squadrons)

The Coalition's named fighter squadrons are its elite units, each based on a capital ship:

|------|------|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Black Guard** (Чёрная Страха) | Eastern Republic | Ivan & Nicolai Petrov | CS Pukov | The most feared unit in the war; "no mercy." Flies the cloaking Basilisk. Killed Steiner & destroyed the Bremen; wiped out by the Yamato + Tigers (M23). |
| **Black Eagles** (Чёрные Орлы) | Eastern Republic | Pytor Lytanov | CS Varyag | The Black Guard's sister squadron; ambush tactics near Jupiter/Mars. *(Lytanov never encountered in-game.)* |
| **Black Sun** (شمس سوداء) | Tigris Confederation | Hakeem Sharif | CS Kozah | War criminals — destroyed the French fleet, hit medical transports, massacred Ganymede. |
| **Golden Warriors** (金勇士) | East Asian Federation | Red Dragon | — | Second only to Black Sun in the EAF; fly Azans; later covert ops / high-security guard. |
| **Saracens** (ساراكينوس) | Tigris Confederation | Al-Rahan | CS Rameses | ~3:1 kill ratio (unconfirmed); took part in the opening Europa strike. |
| **Scorpions** (العقارب) | Tigris Confederation | Kariq Madiz | CS Saladin | Convoy protection; destroyed an Allied hospital ship and its escape pods. War criminals. |
| **Sabers** (Саблі) | Eastern Republic | Vladimir Kaspov | CS Gegarin | Honorable, respected adversaries; mostly volunteers from satellite countries. |
| **Ravens** (Вороны) | Eastern Republic | Katrina Illyanya | CS Borodin | Stealth / hit-and-run; named for the "Midnight Madness" night raid on Lunar command. |

## Locations

### Alliance forts & installations

| Name | Type | Significance |
|------|------|--------------|
| **Base Kennedy** | Colony (Europa) | Major colony; the intended Balma Treaty signing site. Fell to Kulov's fleet in the opening attack. |
| **Fort Sherman** | Fort (off Triton) | Receiving point for refugees and remnants; staging area for the counter-offensive. (M1/M10/M16) |
| **Fort Baxter** | Fort (Neptune) | Major military traffic hub; **destroyed by CS Rameses** (Sep 22 2160). Base of the 205th Marauders. |
| **Fort Carter** | Fort (near Uranus) | Capital-ship repair/refit + logistics; receives the stolen Coalition fuel cells. Base of the 109th Maulers. |
| **Fort Vanguard** | Fort | Gutted early; became McGann's pirate lair (with a captured ion cannon). Cleared in M13. |
| **Fort Bear** | Fort | The first Alliance fort built *after* the war began (2161); staging area for the Saturn/Jupiter push. |
| **Fort Rushmore** | Fort (off Uranus) | Last line before Neptune; fell at the war's nadir (2160); retaken Jan 4 2161. |
| **Fort Lomax** | Fort | Base of the 503rd Stingers after fleeing central Sol. |
| **Fort Austin** | Fort | Base of the 51st Volunteers after fleeing central Sol. |
| **Station LS9** | Listening post (off Neptune) | *Trial-mission only.* Gave early warning of the attack. |

### Coalition installations

The major Coalition installations — **CS Borodin** (the final-mission command station), **Kronstadt**
(supply depot), the **Stalag** (warp-fuel storage), the **Dark Reign** (ion-cannon superweapon), and
the **Latov Asteroid Observation Base** — are detailed with their roles and mission appearances in
[`capital-ships.md` § Installations](capital-ships.md#installations--forts).
